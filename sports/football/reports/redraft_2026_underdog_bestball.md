_12-team · **half ppr** · 1QB / 2RB / 3WR / 1TE / 1FLEX · 18 rounds (216 picks). Features from 2025._

_**The overall board is a pick order — draft down it.** Every projection is model-only; ECR/ADP are benchmarks and never inputs to a model number. The model can't score rookies, so the market (**1QB redraft-overall** ECR) is used for two things and nothing else: how many rookies belong in each position's top-N, and which slot each takes in the order below. Rookie rows are *italic* and carry no projection. The per-position tables are returning players only._

## Best ball: upside is not priced in — on purpose

Fitting the upside weight against 13 walk-forward season pairs (2012-2024) returns **zero at every position**: the Spearman-vs-lambda curve is flat to ~0.003 and no position clears p<0.05. Weekly sigma is ~0.90 rank-correlated with weekly mean at RB/WR/TE, so volatility is mostly a restatement of quality. **Rank by projected PPG; don't pay up for spike-week reputations.** `sigma` below is context, not a tiebreak.
